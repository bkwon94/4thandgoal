AT-18 has no steps.json. The runner's click verb scrolls its target into view first, so it cannot show that the page scrolls the strip back.
scripts/zz-tb-flows.ts.txt (blockedFlow) made each shot, in Chromium at 375x812, 768x1024, 1280x900 in light and dark, and in iPhone WebKit at 390x660 light.
The steps: open Find mode as Bo Girds. Tap Cam Skattebo, Zay Flowers, and Blake Corum (Skattebo stands in for Walker).
Set the scroll area of the strip to its end (scrollTo with behavior "instant"), wait 800 ms, read scrollLeft, take end-<size>-<theme>.png.
Click Kirk Cousins (stand-in for Jackson) with locator.dispatchEvent("click"), a DOM click that does not scroll first. Cousins is a QB, so he sorts second, right after the picks.
Wait 1000 ms, read scrollLeft, read the pressed toggles, the counter, and the full-block line, and take after-<size>-<theme>.png.
