# 05: What does Jev read, what happens when Jev fails, and what does the log show?

Map: `.scratch/evidence-flags/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** spec

**Blocked by:** 01, 02

**Status:** open

## Question

Three small choices that the spec records. The first is the state that Jev reads: the text of the test, its section of `EVIDENCE.md`, the list of files in `evidence/AT-<n>/`, and the screenshots if ticket 01 allows them. The second is the behavior on a `JevError` or a missing `TYPESAFE_API_KEY`: the evidence goes on with no flags, and the log says so. The third is the one log line of each run, with the count of flags, the errors, and the input tokens, in the shape of the weekly build line.
