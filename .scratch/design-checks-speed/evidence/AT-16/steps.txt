main, full-page shots, and the computed fills of both branches (agent-browser, session dcs16):

   1. `agent-browser set viewport <width> 900`, open /, set cpc.theme in local storage, reload, wait 1500 ms.

   5. Move the mouse to (2, 2), then `agent-browser hover 'article ul li a'` (the Season Sim tile), wait 600 ms.

   not kept, because the runner shots above are the Spec full-page shots. The raw output is spec-computed-raw.txt.
10. computed.txt puts the values of both branches side by side.
11. Stop the static server and the API, close the browser, and remove both detached worktrees with `git worktree remove`.
