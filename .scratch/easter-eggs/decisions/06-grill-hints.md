# 06: Does the site tell managers about the eggs

Map: `.scratch/easter-eggs/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** grill

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** done

## Question

Decide if the site tells managers that eggs exist and that six drop each Tuesday. Decide if an egg gives a hint, such as the page it is on, and if a hint gets easier over time. With no word on the site, nobody hunts. With too much, the eggs get easy, and an AI gets help.

## Decision

Brian decided in the grill of 2026-11-06.

- A loud launch: the egg board is in the menu from the first egg drop. Each Tuesday the home page shows a banner with the count of new eggs and the count of eggs left, such as "6 new eggs dropped. 31 left."
- No hints: no egg names its page, its spot, or a riddle. The banner gives only the two counts.

The risk: an egg that nobody finds keeps its letter, so an egg word can stall. Brian accepts it. His fix is a bigger egg drop each week.
