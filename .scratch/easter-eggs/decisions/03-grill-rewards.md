# 03: What the rewards are

Map: `.scratch/easter-eggs/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** grill

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** done

## Question

Brian set three prizes in the grill of 2026-11-06: a gift card for each egg word, CORNERSTONE the biggest, LANSDALE the second, and EAGLES the third. Decide the value of each gift card. Decide if the egg board shows the prizes.

## Decision

Brian decided in the grill of 2026-11-06. Each egg word has one gift card for its first winner: $50 for CORNERSTONE, $30 for LANSDALE, and $20 for EAGLES, $100 in all. The egg board shows the value of each gift card beside the race of its egg word, because a visible prize is a reason to come back. Brian gives each gift card by hand.
