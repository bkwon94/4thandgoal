# 04: Does an egg end with its week

Map: `.scratch/easter-eggs/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** grill

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** done

## Question

Decide if an egg ends when the next week build publishes, or stays hidden until a hunter finds it. A lasting egg piles up eggs on old pages. An ending egg gives a deadline and a weekly reason to visit. The answer sets what the egg board shows and how many eggs are live at once.

## Decision

Brian decided in the grill of 2026-11-06. An egg stays live from its egg drop until its first claim, then it is gone. It does not end with its week. Each egg is single use, and its letter goes to one manager.
