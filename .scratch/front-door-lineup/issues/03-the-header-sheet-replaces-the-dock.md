# 03: The header sheet replaces the dock on the front door

Spec: `.scratch/front-door-lineup/PRD.md`. Acceptance tests AT-45, AT-46, AT-47, AT-48, AT-49, AT-50.

**App:** site

**What to build:** On the front door, the dock and the week pill leave. A menu button at the right of the header opens a sheet of five page rows with notes, a check on the current page, a week row, and "Past weeks". The sheet closes by the X, a tap on the dimmed page, Escape, or a swipe up, and the focus returns to the button. The Suggestions row and the ask box sit on one flush surface at the bottom. The other pages keep the dock and the week pill. On a desktop the pages stay inline in the header.

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The front door at 375 px has no dock and no week pill, and the menu button opens the sheet with the five rows and notes of the spec (AT-45, AT-47).
- [ ] Each of the four close ways closes the sheet, and the focus returns to the button (AT-46).
- [ ] The foot is one surface that touches the bottom edge, and it drops the padding of the home indicator while the keyboard is open (AT-48).
- [ ] The week report page and the rankings page keep the dock and the week pill (AT-49).
- [ ] At 1280 px the header shows the pages inline and no menu button (AT-50).
- [ ] The design bar of the spec ran, and `critique` and `polish` found nothing open.
