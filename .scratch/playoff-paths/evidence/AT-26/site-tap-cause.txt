AT-26: the cause of the second tap in 2 of 8 runs of the ce601c12 recording

Cause: the script. The site needs no code fix.

The front door shows a row of 3 quick chips beside "More". The API picks the 3 chips for each load, so the row changes:
"Holes, Roster, Recap" on most loads, and "Holes, Roster, Playoffs" on some loads. The quick chip "Playoffs" has the
aria-label "Playoff race: 48% playoff odds", the same as the Suggestion "Playoff race" in the More list, and it comes first
in the page. The old selector button[aria-label^='Playoff race'] with .first() then took the quick chip. A first tap on a
quick chip only opens a strip "Playoff race 48% playoff odds  Open →" and closes the More list. This is the design of
apps/site/src/app/front-door/suggestions.tsx, lines 3-4 of its header: "A first tap on a chip shows the full name and the fact
in a strip under the row, and a second tap opens the Topic." So the Answer showed only after the second tap of the retry.

Proof (probe on 5f26bcb0, WebKit, files in this folder):
- site-tap-probe.mjs.txt: the probe. Each trial logs the API calls with times, the box of the tapped element, the element on
  top at the tap point, the React props on the chip, and the topic the thread opened.
- site-tap-probe-390x660-dark.log, trial 1 of 12: the tapped element is 89x44 at 278,233 (the quick chip), not 348x48 at
  21,481 (the More list row). After the tap: "no Answer in 15 s", the page text holds "More Holes Roster Playoffs / Playoff
  race 48% playoff odds / Open →", and the thread opened no topic. Trials 2 to 12 tapped the list row and the Answer showed.
- site-tap-probe-390x660-dark-first.log: the same, trial 5 of 6.
- site-tap-probe-810x980-dark.log: 6 of 6 trials with a tap 30 ms after "More" opened, with no wait: each Answer showed.
- site-tap-probe-trial1-before-tap.png and site-tap-probe-trial1-after-tap.png: the row with "Playoffs" before the tap,
  and the strip with "Open →" after it.
What is not the cause:
- An overlay: elementFromPoint at the tap point is the chip itself in every trial ("overlay": false). The Announcement and
  the Tour are off by their stored keys, and no dialog is open.
- A hydration delay: the chip has React props at the tap in every trial, and a tap 30 ms after "More" opened works.

Fix of the script (evidence/site-set/a/tests.mjs, AT-26): the tap takes the button whose aria-label starts with "Playoff race"
and whose text holds "Playoff race" (the More list row). The quick chip text is "Playoffs", so it no longer matches. The retry
is gone; the run records if the first tap showed the Answer.
Recording again on 5f26bcb0 (files site-5f26bcb0-*): the Answer showed after the first tap in 8 of 8 runs, and 2 of the 8 runs
(1280x800 light and dark) had the quick chip "Playoffs" in the row (site-tries.txt).
