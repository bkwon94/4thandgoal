Overlap check of AT-26, WebKit, client cpc. Rule: no visible text box overlaps another by more than 3 px both ways; at the top of the page no text sits under a bar in the upper half of the viewport; at the end of the page no text sits under a bar in the lower half of the viewport; in an open sheet no text sits under a fixed bar outside the sheet.
