Local storage of each fresh context, written before each load: cpc.theme, cpc.your-team = 6 (Bo Girds, the key YOUR_TEAM_KEY of packages/analyzer/site/lib/your-team.ts), cpc.tour-seen = 1, cpc.announcement.the-race-2026 = seen, and cpc.announcement.season-sim-2026 = seen, unless the steps below say otherwise.
Shot: a scroll pass down and back, a wait of 1.2 s, then a full-page screenshot.
Overlap check: overlapCheck() in lib.mjs, output in overlap.txt. 1: no text line box overlaps a text line box of another element by more than 2 px each way, with each fixed or sticky part hidden (the rule of findOverlap in e2e/smoke.ts). While a modal layer shows, only its texts count. 2: no fixed or sticky bar covers the center of a text box at the top of the page or at the end of the page. 3: the page does not scroll sideways.
Network log: each request, response, and failed request of the context to a host other than the local export.
Command: node evidence/site-set-b/run.mjs <AT-n ...>
