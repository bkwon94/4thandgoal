# 14: What is the flow of the report page, and how much text does each chapter hold?

Map: `.scratch/midseason-report/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** prototype

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** done

## Question

Brian saw the built report on the local preview on 2026-11-06. He found the flow of the page weak, the text cluttered and too wordy, and the look below the refreshed redesign pages. Screenshots: `~/screenshots/midseason-feedback/01` to `06`.

This ticket decides the order of the parts on the page, the parts to merge or cut, and the amount of text in each chapter. Decision 09 set three acts, the title race hero, and the chapter rail. This ticket can keep or change each of them. Brian must see boards with the real CPC week 8 data on the Design canvas before the choice.

## Decision

Brian decided it on 2026-11-06, after four phone boards with the real CPC week 8 data in the row "Ticket 14 · the report flow" of the Design canvas: F1 Lean acts, F2 Digest first, F3 Stakes first, and F4 Mix. F4 took the order of F3, the closed chapters of F2 for the past, and the word cap of F1. He chose F1 Lean acts.

- **The shape stays three acts,** with the title race hero, the act pages, and the sticky rail of decision 09.
- **Each chapter is a headline, one visual or table, and 40 words of prose or fewer.** On the F1 board the largest chapter has 34 words, and the report has 365 in total.
- **Lineup choices and injury toll fold into one "Quick hits" block** at the end of Act II.
- **The sim stays the finale of Act III.** The hero adds a link, "See the sim", that jumps to it. The hero already shows the title odds, so a manager sees the stakes at the top. The replay of decision 13 is built as an ending.
- **The page stays long,** about 10,000 px on a phone. Brian: if the page looks and feels great, with good content, people read it. No chapter closes by default.
- The spread rule of decision 09 holds: each team gets 1 to 3 headlines. The F1 board meets it.

Not chosen: the digest of 10 facts of F2, because it repeats the chapters. The stakes-first order of F3 and F4, because the report then has no ending and the acts lose their order.

The boards show the look of the rows, the tags, the swap, and the roast only as samples. Tickets 15, 16, and 17 decide them.
