# 16: How does the schedule swap show on the page?

Map: `.scratch/midseason-report/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** prototype

**Blocked by:** 14

**Status:** done

## Question

Brian found the 12 by 12 grid of the schedule swap awful. Decision 06 set the grid and its rules: the self-meet rule, and the median game in the CPC cells. This ticket decides how the swap shows: the grid in a new look, or another form of the same facts. The rules of decision 06 stay. Brian must see boards with the real data before the choice.

## Decision

Brian decided it on 2026-11-06, after four phone boards with the real CPC week 8 data in the row "Ticket 16 · the schedule swap" of the Design canvas: S1 Quiet grid, S2 One team at a time, S3 Best and worst, and S3 v2. He chose S3 v2 with row form A.

- **The swap is a list of best and worst, not a grid.** One row per team: the real record, the best record and whose schedule gives it, and the worst record and whose schedule gives it, with the change in wins as a muted word (+5, −1, same). Rows sort by the spread between best and worst. A tie of schedules reads "or" for 2 teams, and "and N others" for more.
- **No bar.** Brian could not tell what the range bar showed. The row is text only.
- **One plain sentence opens the swap:** "Each row puts your scores against another team's schedule. Here is your best and your worst." A small line says the swap counts all 16 games, 8 head to head and 8 median games, and that best and worst use the 11 other schedules.
- **The page never says "All-play".** Brian did not know what it meant. The luck table columns are "Real wins", "Wins if you played everyone", and "Luck (gap)". A short card above the table explains the idea before any number, with a worked example from one real team. The 61 words of the card are fine.
- **Each luck number has a word:** "lucky" above 0, "unlucky" below 0, and "even" for a gap smaller than 0.5 wins. The word takes the accent color at +1.5 or more, or −1.5 or less, the lines of decision 06.
- **The spread rule stays.** The callouts keep the choice of the spread rule, so no headline names Bo Girds for 14-2. The S3 row of Bo Girds still shows it.
- The rules of decision 06 stay: the self-meet rule, and the median game in the CPC cells.

Not chosen: S1, because it is still a grid. S2, because a switch of 12 teams hides 11 of them.
