## Question

The report has 14 chapters, and a phone reader scrolls them all. The spec needs the order, the hero of the report, the look of each chapter in the redesign, and which chapters merge, such as luck with the schedule swap. Use the redesign boards on the Design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/4cFaYVh4Sm9Cw68G7FEds8 as the base. Brian chooses on the canvas.

## Decision

Brian decided it on 2026-11-05, after three phone boards with real CPC week 8 data in the row "Ticket 09 · the midseason report" of the Design canvas: R1 Story, R2 Three acts, and R3 Cover and index. He chose R2, with the hero and the chapter rail of R1.

- **The shape is three acts.** Each act opens with a dark title page. A sticky chapter rail at the top jumps to each act.
- **The hero is the title race,** from R1: no team owns the title, the title odds of the leaders, and the record leader's low title odds. Every manager has a stake in it. A hero about one team, such as the luckiest team or the top Robbery, interests one manager.
- **The order:**
  - Act I, "How we got here": the state of the league, arcs, schedule luck with the schedule swap, injury toll, Position top 10.
  - Act II, "The moves": draft steals and busts, waiver hits and misses, the trade report card, lineup choices, the record book with the awards.
  - Act III, "What comes next": the look ahead, players to watch, and the sim as the finale.
- **Two merges.** Schedule luck and the schedule swap are one chapter: the luck table, then the grid. The record book and the awards are one chapter. Heartbreak is the closest game and Beatdown is the biggest margin, so each game shows once.
- **The look of each chapter** follows the R2 board. The trade top 3 and the awards are swipe cards. Position top 10 has the position switch of ticket 10.
- **The spread rule.** Each team gets at least one headline: the hero, a callout, an award, or an arc. No team holds more than 3 headlines. When a team is #1 in a fourth place, the callout names the next team, and the table still shows every team. The data made Bo Girds the extreme team in many chapters: R1 named it 22 times, and other teams 9 to 17 times.
- R3's tile with one big fact is the shape for the home tile of ticket 11. A report of tiles needs too many taps.
