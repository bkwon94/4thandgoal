# Notes: candidate Product names, checked 2026-11-11

This file lists 15 candidate names for the fantasy football AI expert that replaces "4th & Goal". Each name has a check of the domains, the GitHub name, and live clashes in fantasy sports. Brian picks the name. The ranks below are advice only.

Method:

- Domains: the RDAP server of each registry. Verisign for `.com`, Google Registry for `.app`, and Identity Digital for `.ai`. A 404 means "not registered". A 200 means "taken". The public `rdap.org` proxy rate-limited the run with 429, so the run went to the registries direct.
- Price at cost, Cloudflare Registrar: `.com` $10.46 per year. `.app` $8.20 for the first year, then $14.20. `.ai` $80.00 per year. The `.ai` registry sells a 2-year minimum, so the first `.ai` bill is about $160.
- GitHub: `https://github.com/<name>`. A 200 means a user or an org holds the name.
- Clash: web search and the domain page of each taken domain.
- USPTO: no check. The `tmsearch.uspto.gov` search needs a browser session. Brian or a later ticket must run it on the final 2 or 3 names.

## Top 5

1. **Ask Huddle**: says the job in two words. It has a football word and a free `.com`, `.app`, and `.ai`. No live product has the name.
2. **Huddle Sage**: says "expert" with a football word. All three domains are free, and no clash came up.
3. **Pocket OC**: the most football of the set. It reads as "your play caller in your pocket". All domains are free. Risk: casual managers may not know "OC".
4. **League Oracle**: says "ask about your league, get answers". All domains are free. Risk: Gridiron Oracle is a near name in sports bets.
5. **Grid Genie**: coined and playful, with "ask anything" in the word "genie". `.app` and `.ai` are free. The `.com` is taken but shows no site, so a buy may be possible.
