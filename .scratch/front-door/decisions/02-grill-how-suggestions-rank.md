# 02: How is a Suggestion picked and put in order?

Map: `.scratch/front-door/MAP.md`.

**Kind:** grill

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** done

## Question

Decide how the front door picks 3 to 5 Suggestions and their order. Inputs can be the day of the week, Your team, a big event such as a sell-high chance, and the Topics the manager already saw. The spec needs the rule, because the Suggestions are what most managers see first.

## Decision

Brian, 2026-11-09, in a grill.

1. The day of the week sets the base order, because the calendar is a hard fact. For example, the recap comes first on Tuesday. Jev then lifts a Topic when a fact makes it urgent for Your team, such as odds on the bubble, a clear sell-high, or a starter who is out. Reason: code keeps hard rules, and Jev judges.
2. Each Suggestion shows the name of its Topic plus one fact from code, such as "Your weak spots: RB ranks 11th of 12". It is the same fact that Jev reads to lift the Topic. It costs nothing and needs no check. Reason: a real number gets a tap, and a Claude line would add a call and a wait to each visit.
3. The front door shows 3 Suggestions plus a "More" row that lists all 13 Topics. A Topic that the manager opened drops off until its fact changes or the week turns. The browser keeps that memory next to Your team. Reason: three fit on a phone above the ask box, and "More" loses no Topic.
