# AT-56: the page.json of CPC 2026 week 8, built on front-door (99d00125) and on main (1d7bfc0d), each in its own detached worktree.
# What runs: make page-json on each branch. It writes page.json from the saved facts and blurbs of week 8 with no writer call and no network, so neither run refetches Sleeper or writes new Claude text.
