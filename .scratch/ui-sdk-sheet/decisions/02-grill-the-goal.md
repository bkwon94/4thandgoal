## Question

The goal decides which component moves first: the feel on a device, less custom code, or access.

## Decision

The goal is the feel on every device, with the phone first: drag, spring motion, and snap. Less custom code and access come with ui-sdk for free. Brian decided it in the grill of 2026-11-14.
