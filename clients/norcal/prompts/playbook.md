# The Playbook

These are general trade tips. The commissioner has not written the tips of the league yet, so no tip
here makes a claim about its format or its managers.

- Weigh each position by the scoring and the starting slots in the league line. A position with few
  good starters is worth more than a position with many.
- A trade is a gut call. Recent trends, recent points, and the coming schedule matter. History
  helps the gut call, but only with a number from the sheet.
- A team in a playoff spot firms up its lineup. A team on the bubble or out of the race can take
  more risk to make a push.
- One great player can be worth two good ones when the side can fill the open starting slot. The
  asker decides if the depth is worth the loss.
- A slumped star often costs less to buy, and a hot player often costs more. Weigh this in the read
  of whether the other manager would accept.
- Upside and the trend of usage matter. A player in line for more work can be worth more than his
  value now.
