# Vocabulary

These are the only stat terms you may use. Each term means exactly what its line says. Do not use a loose word for a defined stat.

- **Top Scorer**: the starter with the most points. League-wide, it is the starter with the most points on any team.
- **MVB**: the bench player with the most points, across all teams.
- **Underperformer**: the starter with the largest gap below projection, among starters projected at 10 or more.
- **Overachiever**: the starter with the largest gap above projection.
- **Game Changer**: the starter on a winning side whose points exceed the margin, ranked by gap to projection, with a bonus for a recent add.
- **Matchup of the Week**: the matchup that scored highest on closest margin, biggest upset, highest combined standing, and nearness to the playoff cut.
- **margin driver**: a player on either side whose gap to projection, in absolute terms, is at least the margin. Only a margin driver may be called a decider.
- **swing player**: a player on the winning side whose points exceed the margin. Say "without X, that is a loss" only for a swing player.
- **bench regret**: a bench player who, swapped for the lowest starter at the same position, would have flipped the result.
- **median game**: the extra game every team plays against the league median score. A week is two games: the head-to-head and the median game.
- **Must Watch**: a top one or two matchup next week, chosen from records and standings only.
- **next opponent win rate**: the win rate of a team's opponent next week, with the league rank of that number, toughest first. It covers next week only, not the rest of the season.
- **position rank**: a starter's rank among all starters at the same position this week.
- **streak**: head-to-head results only, the median game excluded.
- **team trend**: the last 3 weeks against the season average.
- **player trend**: against projection over the last 3 weeks.
- **season high**: a team's highest weekly total this season.
- **season low**: a team's lowest weekly total this season.
- **season average**: a team's points for divided by the weeks played this season.
- **roster need**: depth at the traded positions and open spots, as of this week's lineup.
- **trade swing**: the gap against the margin. Only when the gap is at least the margin may the writer say the trade decided the game.
- **trade grade**: an opinion from the fact sheet, A to F, with the reason.
- **upset**: the winner ranked below the loser in the standings before this week. Use it only in a sentence that cites a fact with `.upset` in its ID.
- **recent add**: a starter added by waiver, free agent, or trade in this week or the week before. Use it, and the word "lucky", only in a sentence that cites a fact with `.move.` in its ID or the fact `W.adds`.
- **injury status**: the Sleeper designation for a player as of the Tuesday after the games. It says nothing about the player's status at kickoff. Use it only in a sentence that cites a fact with `.inj.` in its ID or the fact `W.injured`.
- **drop**: a pass the FTN charting flags as a drop. With nflverse down, the count is Sleeper's `rec_drop`.
- **drop value**: an estimate. One reception at the league rate, plus the air yards at the per-yard rate, plus a touchdown when the throw reached the end zone.
- **drop swing**: the losing side's drop value is at least the margin. Only then may the writer say the drops cost the game.
- **Butterfingers**: the started player with the most drops, across all teams.

## Banned words

Never write these words: best, star, carried, hero, MVP, clutch, exploded, cooked.
