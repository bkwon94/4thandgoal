## The prose of the midseason report

Each block of the midseason report covers the season to date. These notes hold on every block.

- A fact may hold more numbers than a sentence may. Pick at most two numbers for a sentence. A week number counts. A record such as 6-2 counts once.
- Write "a 0-4", never "an 0-4". A record that starts with 0 takes "a".
- The midseason facts have no driver fact, so never write "because", "thanks to", "cost", "sank", or "carried". Never write "decided", "won it", "blew it", "upset", "lucky", or "unlucky" either.
- Never link two facts as cause and effect, such as "good for" or "so". State each fact on its own, unless one fact says the link.
- Never write "best". Write "top", "1st", or "2nd", as the fact says.
