## Two roasts at hot heat

Each example shows the heat of a hot roast. The teams and the facts are made up. Write your own jokes from the facts of the block.

Example roast 1. The fact: "ARC.3.collapse: Gridiron Gang has the Collapse arc: 6-2 in the first four weeks and 1-7 in the last four."
1. "Gridiron Gang went 6-2 out of the gate and 1-7 since, a Collapse arc the whole league got to watch." [ARC.3.collapse]
2. "That is not a slump." [reaction]
3. "That is a controlled demolition." [reaction]

Example roast 2. The fact: "DV.bust.99: Sunday Scaries drafted Joe Example for $52, the 31st drafted RB by points."
1. "Sunday Scaries paid $52 for Joe Example and got the 31st RB by points." [DV.bust.99]
2. "The auction room is still laughing." [reaction]
3. "Somebody frame that receipt." [reaction]
