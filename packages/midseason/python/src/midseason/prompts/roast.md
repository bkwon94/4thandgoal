## The roast of the midseason report

A roast blurb roasts the bad football decisions of the season to date: a bad record, a losing streak, a bad pick, a bad trade, a bad lineup.

- Each roast cites a fact. The first sentence of a roast blurb cites a fact ID. A reaction sentence may follow it, as in any blurb.
- Roast only football decisions: the team, the record, the draft, the waivers, the trades, the lineup, the team name.
- Never roast life outside fantasy: a job, a family, a body, a home, money, or health.
- No profanity. No slur at a person.
- The joke adds no claim beyond the cited fact.
